Before you begin

This is not meant to grade your PLC or evaluate individual teachers.

It is meant to help each person pause and think: What conditions are helping us work as a true professional learning community, and what conditions may need attention?

Answer based on what you have actually experienced in your most recent PLC meetings. When possible, think of a concrete example before choosing a rating.

Privacy note: This page does not store or transmit your responses. Your results stay in your browser unless you choose to copy and share your response code with a facilitator.

Rating Scale

1 → Not Yet Evident
I do not see this happening yet, or it happens so rarely that it does not shape our PLC work.
2 → Emerging
This happens sometimes, but it is inconsistent or depends on the situation/person.
3 → Developing
This is present and helpful, but we could make it more intentional or consistent.
4 → Established
This is a regular part of how our PLC works, and it supports meaningful collaboration.
