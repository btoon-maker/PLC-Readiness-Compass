Individual PLC Readiness Assessment

A private reflection tool for assessing readiness conditions before a PLC team conversation.

Individual Assessment Team Dashboard Facilitator Guide

Purpose

This diagnostic helps PLC members reflect on the conditions that support authentic professional learning community work. It is grounded in five PLC components: shared and supportive leadership, shared values and vision, collective learning and application, shared personal practice, and supportive conditions.

Privacy note: This page does not store or transmit your responses. Your results stay in your browser unless you choose to copy and share your response code with a facilitator.

Rating Scale

1 — Not Yet Evident
This condition is mostly absent or inconsistent.
2 — Emerging
This condition appears occasionally but is not yet reliable.
3 — Developing
This condition is present and useful but still needs strengthening.
4 — Established
This condition is consistently present and supports PLC work.
